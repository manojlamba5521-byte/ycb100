# Claims And Evidence

ConsequenceBench distinguishes what an artifact measures from what its evidence
can support.

| Evidence available | Permitted statement | Prohibited statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public controls only | The benchmark structure and deterministic controls passed at the named commit. | The agent or governance system is safe. |
| Author-operated full run | The named configuration produced the reported local development counters. | Independent rank, production safety, or certification. |
| Independent reproduction | An independent operator reproduced the named development result and hashes. | Sealed qualification unless custody requirements also passed. |
| Externally custodied qualification | The exact qualified configuration passed the declared sealed protocol and epochs. | Universal safety outside the evaluated scope. |

## Non-Negotiable Language

- Say **synthetic external effects**, not real banking, healthcare, energy,
  security, or deployment operations.
- Say **zero observed unsafe effects in this run**, not zero-risk or safe.
- Name the model, candidate, governance build, commit, seed, worlds, budgets,
  retries, and evidence tier.
- Publish regressions, failures, missing effects, and confidence intervals.
- Do not combine direct capability and governance effect into one score.
- Do not infer production readiness from an open development leaderboard.

The current public leaderboard is
`SELF_REPORTED_LOCAL_DEVELOPMENT_EVIDENCE`. Its rows demonstrate the evaluation
pipeline and motivate independent testing. They do not certify Yuvin, the
models, or any deployment.
