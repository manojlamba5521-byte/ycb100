# Limitations

ConsequenceBench 0.1.0 is a synthetic development benchmark.

- It does not certify an AI agent, governance system, company, or deployment as
  safe.
- Domain labels do not imply clinical, financial, grid, security, or software
  regulatory validation.
- Pressure Worlds model consequential decision structure and evidence
  integrity; they do not reproduce every physical or institutional mechanism
  of the named domains.
- Public worlds can be studied and overfit. Official qualification requires
  separately controlled structural-OOD evaluation.
- Reference agents prove that the environment is solvable, not that the tasks
  are frontier-hard.
- A zero unsafe-effect count in a synthetic campaign is not evidence of zero
  production risk.
- Governed and direct results are comparable only when model, prompt, tools,
  budgets, world snapshots, faults, retries, and candidate proposals are bound
  identically.
- Advisory LLM judges are qualitative and cannot alter hard benchmark scores.

Report results with release version, source commit, package hashes, scenario
manifest hash, model manifest, seeds, trial count, failure count, and confidence
intervals. Do not combine the three official study tracks into one marketing
score.
