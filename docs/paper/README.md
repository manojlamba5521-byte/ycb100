# Research notes

This directory contains scoped, preliminary research notes. They are published
with the source repository for discussion and review; they are not benchmark
qualification artifacts or product documentation.

## Governance-layer note

[Runtime Policy Gates May Prevent Harm in Simple Worlds; Consequence Closure
Requires Durability Tests](GOVERNANCE_IS_NOT_OPTIONAL.md) records a small,
self-reported local study. Its principal correction is methodological: the
study contains two AGT-versus-Yuvin head-to-head cells, not four. The paper also
states that its AGT integration includes locally written source readback and
that the complete AGT evidence bundle is not public.

The paper is exported in the deterministic public-source archive, but its raw
run evidence is not. Its claims must therefore be read under the repository's
[claims-and-evidence policy](../CLAIMS_AND_EVIDENCE.md) and must not be used for
product selection, regulatory analysis, or a safety claim.
