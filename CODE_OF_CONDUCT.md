# Code of Conduct

YCB-100 contributors must maintain a professional, evidence-oriented research
environment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private
evaluation material, credential sharing, benchmark sabotage, and personal
attacks are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome when it is
specific, reproducible, and directed at claims or implementations rather than
people.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior threatens contributors, users, research integrity, or evaluator
security. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers
or GitHub's reporting mechanisms.
