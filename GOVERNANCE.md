# Project Governance

YCB-100 is maintained by the Yuvin Research Team.

Maintainers control executable contracts, public releases, scoring schemas, and
qualification claims. No individual contributor, model provider, candidate
system author, or Yuvin product owner may unilaterally certify a benchmark
release.

Changes to hard counters, oracle logic, candidate visibility, catalog identity,
or qualification thresholds require:

1. A public design rationale.
2. Regression evidence.
3. A versioned schema or protocol change when compatibility is affected.
4. Review by a maintainer who did not author the implementation.

Official qualification additionally requires independent evaluator custody and
the evidence listed in `docs/YCB100_QUALIFICATION_PLAN.md`.
